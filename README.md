### About Me

I am a Senior Software Engineer and Systems Architect out of the Caribbean, Trinidad & Tobago to be exact. I have spent over a decade working across Energy, IoTs, Logistics, Manufacturing, Medical and more recently Aviation. These days I primarily work on backend microservices, cloud infrastructure and kiosk systems, but I still carry my Full Stack and Machine Learning history with me. I enjoy spearheading projects alongside my teams - whether I am at the helm or not.

My growth has been a bit of a journey --- I started out tinkering with Arduino and school projects as an absolute newbie, moved into C++ firmware and IoT hardware, then into full stack work alongside growing engineering teams. From there I went on to architect backend microservices and data layers in healthcare, and today I work as a Senior Software Engineer and Systems Architect in aviation - mostly with async Python, Flutter, and Linux - with some leadership responsibility for the team and the architecture we build on.
Outside of the day job I lecture Python, OOP, Git and Machine Learning to undergraduate and postgraduate students. On my own time I have been learning Rust the hands-on way - building a SQL database engine from scratch (column indexing, SIMD search, zero-copy serialization) and an encrypted incremental backup CLI (Rabin fingerprinting, Merkle trees, ChaCha20-Poly1305).

Most of my work still lives on BitBucket or on internal hosts, so this GitHub stays fairly light.

I also love Astronomy, so on a clear night you might find me outside with my Seestar S50 telescope - and afterwards delving into image processing pipelines to stack and clean up whatever I captured.

In my spare time you might also find me playing Dungeons & Dragons - [check out my Dice Rolling CLI here!](https://github.com/GerardRagbir/DungeonDice-CLI) - it started as a quick attempt a few years ago, and I finally got around to giving it a proper update, so it's much nicer now!
